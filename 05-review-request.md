# 05 – Review Request

## Purpose

This prompt generates a polite, friendly, and professional email requesting a customer review or testimonial after they have successfully received a product or service. It encourages customer feedback without being pushy, helping businesses build trust, improve their reputation, and gather valuable insights for future improvements.

---

## Prompt

You are a professional Customer Success Representative.

Write a warm, friendly, and professional email asking a customer to leave a review after completing their purchase or using our service.

The email should:
- Thank the customer for choosing our company.
- Express appreciation for their business.
- Politely ask them to leave a review or testimonial.
- Explain how their feedback helps both the business and future customers.
- Include a placeholder for a review link.
- End with a sincere thank-you and friendly closing.

Use an appreciative, conversational, and customer-focused tone.

### Customer Information

- Customer Name: {{customer_name}}
- Product or Service: {{product_or_service}}
- Purchase Date: {{purchase_date}}
- Review Link: {{review_link}}

---

## Variables

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `{{customer_name}}` | Name of the customer |
| `{{product_or_service}}` | Product purchased or service received |
| `{{purchase_date}}` | Date of purchase or service completion |
| `{{review_link}}` | URL where the customer can leave a review |

---

## Why this prompt works

This prompt balances gratitude with a respectful request for feedback, making the email feel genuine rather than promotional. By thanking the customer first, explaining the value of their review, and keeping the request brief and low-pressure, the AI consistently generates messages that encourage engagement while maintaining a positive customer experience. The reusable variables also make the prompt easy to customize for different businesses, products, and services.

---

## Example Input

**Customer Name:** Emily Carter

**Product or Service:** Premium Website Maintenance Plan

**Purchase Date:** July 8, 2026

**Review Link:** https://example.com/review

---

## Example Output

**Subject:** We'd Love to Hear Your Feedback!

Hi Emily,

Thank you for choosing our **Premium Website Maintenance Plan**. We truly appreciate your trust in us and hope you've had a great experience since your purchase on **July 8, 2026**.

If you have a few moments, we'd be grateful if you could share your experience by leaving a review. Your feedback helps us continue improving our services and also helps other customers make informed decisions.

You can leave your review here:

**https://example.com/review**

Thank you once again for your support. We genuinely appreciate your business and look forward to serving you again in the future.

Warm regards,

Customer Success Team

---

## Notes

- Replace the placeholder variables with the customer's actual information before generating the email.
- Send the review request after the customer has had enough time to use the product or service.
- Keep the message short, appreciative, and free of pressure to encourage authentic feedback.
- This prompt can be adapted for product reviews, service testimonials, Google reviews, Trustpilot reviews, or feedback surveys by changing the review link and wording as needed.
