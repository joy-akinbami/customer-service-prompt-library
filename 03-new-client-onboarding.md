# 03 – New Client Onboarding

## Purpose

This prompt generates a professional and welcoming onboarding email for new clients after they have signed up for a product or service. It helps create a positive first impression by expressing appreciation, outlining the next steps, setting clear expectations, and encouraging open communication throughout the onboarding process.

---

## Prompt

You are a professional Customer Success Specialist.

Write a warm, friendly, and professional onboarding email for a new client.

The email should:
- Thank the client for choosing our company.
- Welcome them to the service.
- Briefly explain what happens next.
- List any important next steps or onboarding milestones.
- Encourage the client to ask questions if they need assistance.
- End with a positive and reassuring closing.

Use a confident, customer-focused tone.

### Client Information

- Client Name: {{client_name}}
- Company: {{company_name}}
- Service Purchased: {{service}}
- Onboarding Call Date: {{onboarding_date}}
- Customer Success Manager: {{manager_name}}

---

## Variables

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `{{client_name}}` | Name of the client |
| `{{company_name}}` | Client's company or organization |
| `{{service}}` | Product or service purchased |
| `{{onboarding_date}}` | Scheduled onboarding meeting or start date |
| `{{manager_name}}` | Customer Success representative assigned to the client |

---

## Why this prompt works

This prompt establishes a welcoming and professional tone while providing enough structure for the AI to produce consistent onboarding emails. Rather than simply welcoming the client, it guides the model to include appreciation, clear expectations, actionable next steps, and an invitation for ongoing communication. Using reusable variables makes the prompt adaptable across different industries and customer types without changing its overall structure.

---

## Example Input

**Client Name:** Jessica Brown

**Company:** BrightPath Consulting

**Service Purchased:** Premium CRM Implementation Package

**Onboarding Call Date:** July 15, 2026, at 10:00 AM

**Customer Success Manager:** Daniel Lewis

---

## Example Output

**Subject:** Welcome to Our Team — Let's Get Started!

Hi Jessica,

Welcome to our team, and thank you for choosing us to support BrightPath Consulting with your Premium CRM Implementation Package.

We're excited to work with you and are committed to making your onboarding experience as smooth and successful as possible.

Here's what you can expect over the next few days:

- Your account setup will be completed before your onboarding session.
- Your onboarding call is scheduled for **July 15, 2026, at 10:00 AM**.
- During the session, we'll walk you through the platform, answer your questions, and help you get everything configured for your team's needs.
- After the meeting, you'll receive additional resources and ongoing support from your dedicated Customer Success Manager.

If you have any questions before your onboarding session, simply reply to this email. We're always happy to help.

We're looking forward to partnering with you and helping your team get the most out of the platform.

Best regards,

Daniel Lewis  
Customer Success Manager

---

## Notes

- Replace the placeholder variables with real client information before generating the email.
- The prompt can be adapted for SaaS platforms, consulting firms, agencies, e-commerce services, or any business with a client onboarding process.
- Adjust the tone to match your organization's brand voice while keeping the message clear, welcoming, and customer-focused.
- If additional onboarding resources (such as documentation, tutorials, or support links) are available, include them after the onboarding steps for a more comprehensive welcome email.
