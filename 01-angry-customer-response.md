# Prompt: Angry Customer Response

   ## The Prompt
   "You are a customer support agent. Write a calm, empathetic email 
   responding to a customer who is frustrated about {issue}. 
   Acknowledge their frustration in the first line, avoid corporate 
   jargon, offer a concrete next step, and keep it under 150 words."

   ## Why it's structured this way
   - Acknowledging frustration first reduces defensiveness before 
     offering a solution
   - The word limit forces conciseness, which reads as more sincere
   - Explicitly banning "corporate jargon" avoids generic-sounding replies

   ## Variables
   - {issue} — short description of what went wrong
