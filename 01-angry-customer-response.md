# Prompt: Angry Customer Response

   ## The Prompt
   "You are a customer support agent. Write a calm, empathetic email 
   responding to a customer who is frustrated about {issue}. 
   Acknowledge their frustration in the first line, avoid corporate 
   jargon, offer a concrete next step, and keep it under 150 words."

   ## Why it's structured this way
   - Acknowledging frustration first reduces defensiveness before 
     offering a solution
   - The word limit forces conciseness, which reads as more sincere
   - Explicitly banning "corporate jargon" avoids generic-sounding replies

   ## Variables
   - {issue} — short description of what went wrong

   ## Example Output
   [Example Output

Subject: We're Sorry About Your Experience

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for reaching out and for sharing your concerns. I'm truly sorry to hear about your experience and understand how frustrating this situation must have been.

After reviewing your order, I can confirm that the delay was caused by an unexpected shipping issue on our end. I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience this has caused.

We've already arranged for your replacement order to be shipped with priority delivery, and you'll receive a tracking email shortly.

We appreciate your patience and the opportunity to make this right. If you have any additional questions, simply reply to this email and I'll be happy to assist.

Best regards,
Customer Support]

## Notes
Adjust tone slightly warmer if the customer is a long-term/VIP client.
