## Purpose
Politely remind a customer about an unpaid invoice without sounding aggressive or damaging the relationship.

## Prompt
"You are an accounts team member. Write a polite but firm follow-up email reminding {{customer_name}} that invoice {{invoice_number}} for was due on {{due_date}} and remains unpaid. Keep the tone professional, avoid sounding threatening, and end with a clear call to action."

## Variables
{{customer_name}}
{{invoice_number}}
{{due_date}}

## Why this prompt works
Specifying "firm but polite" prevents the AI from sounding either too soft (ignored) or too aggressive (damages the relationship). Requiring a clear call-to-action ensures the email actually drives payment.

## Example Input
Customer Name: Michael Obi
Invoice Number: #INV-2291
Due Date: July 20, 2026

## Example Output
Subject: Friendly Reminder – Invoice #2291

Hi Michael,

I hope you're doing well.

This is a friendly reminder that Invoice #2045, issued on June 10, is now five days overdue. We understand schedules can get busy, so we wanted to check in in case it was simply overlooked.

If payment has already been made, please disregard this message. Otherwise, we'd appreciate it if you could process the payment at your earliest convenience.

If you have any questions or need another copy of the invoice, please let us know.

Thank you for your continued business.

Best regards,
Accounts Team

## Notes
For repeat late payers, consider a slightly firmer version as a v2.
